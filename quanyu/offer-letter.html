Compensation Structure

Compensation is structured in three progressive phases to reflect the nature of the engagement:

Phase Period Monthly Base Salary Engagement Level
Phase 1 Months 1 – 2 NT$90,000 50% — Strategy & Assessment
Phase 2 Months 3 – 4 NT$140,000 75% — Transition & Build
Phase 3 Month 5 onward NT$180,000 100% — Full-time COO
Annual Bonus: There is no guaranteed year-end bonus. Any bonus award is entirely at the discretion of the Board of Directors and CEO, subject to company performance and individual KPI achievement as evaluated at the end of each calendar year.
Key Responsibilities
  • 1. Group Operations Integration
    • Establish and oversee the venue general manager system across all locations
    • Establish and oversee the sports division director system
    • Lead weekly operations meetings and monthly business reviews
    • Track group-wide revenue, cost, and member lifecycle metrics
    • Develop and standardize replicable operating procedures (SOP)
    • Design cross-venue and cross-program member referral flows
  • 2. Sports Program Productization
    • Oversee curriculum and product design across all sports verticals (Basketball, Tennis, Pickleball, Sports Recovery)
    • Ensure product-market fit, quality standards, and tiered membership structures
    • Drive member lifecycle design and cross-program upsell and referral
  • 3. CrossFit / HYROX Business Line
    • Act as interim Division Director during the initial launch phase
    • Lead venue positioning, equipment configuration, and curriculum design
    • Recruit, train, and manage coaches for CrossFit and HYROX programs
    • Establish trial class processes, sales flows, and in-house competition systems
  • 4. Events, Competitions & Brand Partnerships
    • Organize inter-program competitions and member challenge events
    • Develop the Quanyu Group challenge event platform
    • Lead corporate wellness program development and enterprise sales
    • Identify and develop external brand partnership opportunities
  • 5. AI & Information Systems Implementation
    • Automate venue manager weekly reports and key operational workflows
    • Implement member CRM and trial-client tracking systems
    • Deploy operational data dashboard and churn prediction tools
    • Build AI-powered FAQ automation and customer service flows
    • Implement the AI Coach Teaching Evaluation System: coaches wear microphones during class; voice and video are recorded and analyzed by AI to evaluate teaching quality, instruction clarity, and coaching style; outputs are used for coach-member matching and periodic coach performance review
    • Lead AI literacy training for venue managers and division directors
  • 6. Executive Reporting
    • Provide regular operational progress and strategic updates to the CEO
    • Drive data-informed decision-making across all business lines
Required Authority & Resources

To fulfill the above responsibilities effectively, the Company agrees to provide the following:

  • Direct management authority over all venue general managers and division directors
  • Full access to venue-level financial and operational data across all locations
  • Budget authority for operational expenditures (scope to be defined within 30 days of start)
  • Authority to propose organizational structure changes for CEO approval
  • Access to all internal operational and information systems

  4. Any conflict of interest involving related entities (including but not limited to 四季健身 / 4SC CrossFit) must be disclosed to the CEO and governed by individually negotiated terms. Neither party assumes the other's obligations or liabilities without explicit written agreement.

Addendum — Points for Clarification Prior to Signing

The COO acknowledges the offer terms in principle and is prepared to sign upon written confirmation of the following seven points by the CEO or authorized representative of Quanyu Group:

1
Automatic Salary Progression
The three-phase salary increases (NT$90,000 → NT$140,000 → NT$180,000) shall take effect automatically upon reaching the specified month milestones, without requiring additional review or approval, provided there are no material performance issues on record.
2
Phase 1–2 KPI Scope
During Months 1–4, performance evaluation shall be based on process and deliverable KPIs — including operational assessment outputs, system and structural build progress, and organizational framework proposals — and shall not apply outcome-based group-level revenue or membership targets that presuppose full authority and full-time engagement.
3
Authority–Responsibility Alignment
The COO's budget, headcount, data access, and cross-departmental management authority shall be formally defined and communicated to all relevant parties prior to or concurrent with the commencement date, and shall be proportionate to the scope of responsibilities assigned at each phase.
4
CrossFit / HYROX New Business Line Compensation
Building the CrossFit / HYROX business from zero constitutes work that goes beyond standard COO management responsibilities. Should this line reach defined operational milestones, both parties agree to separately negotiate a performance bonus or profit-sharing arrangement for this specific business line. Terms to be discussed prior to or during Month 3.
5
Independence of 四季健身 / 4SC CrossFit
四季健身 and 4SC CrossFit remain independent legal and operational entities. This appointment does not constitute — and shall not be construed as — any transfer of brand assets, member data, coach relationships, curriculum systems, operating procedures, or event IP from these entities to Quanyu Group.
6
Written Authorization for Use of 四季 Resources
Any future engagement by Quanyu Group involving 四季健身 or 4SC CrossFit brand, membership, coaches, curriculum, SOP, or event IP shall require a separate written agreement specifying: authorized scope, collaboration period, cost allocation, revenue sharing, and exit mechanism. No such use shall be inferred from this employment relationship.
7
AI Coach Evaluation System — Data Governance
Prior to deployment, the AI Coach Teaching Evaluation System shall be governed by a separately established policy covering: data collection scope (audio / video), coach and member notification and consent procedures, data storage period and location, permitted use scope, access control and authorization, and data deletion protocol. This policy shall comply with applicable laws of the Republic of China (Taiwan) and shall be agreed upon by both parties before system activation.

Upon written acknowledgment of the above seven points, this offer letter shall be considered complete and ready for formal execution. We look forward to welcoming you to the Quanyu Group leadership team.
