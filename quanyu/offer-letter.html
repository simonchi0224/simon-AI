Compensation Structure

Compensation is structured in three progressive phases to reflect the nature of the engagement:

Phase Period Monthly Base Salary Engagement Level
Phase 1 Months 1 – 2 NT$90,000 50% — Strategy & Assessment
Phase 2 Months 3 – 4 NT$140,000 75% — Transition & Build
Phase 3 Month 5 onward NT$180,000 100% — Full-time COO
Annual Bonus: There is no guaranteed fixed-amount year-end bonus. This does not mean no bonus is paid — the annual bonus shall be calculated and paid based on the COO's degree of KPI achievement and company performance, as evaluated at the end of each calendar year.
Key Responsibilities
  • 1. Group Operations Integration
    • Establish and oversee the venue general manager system across all locations
    • Establish and oversee the sports division director system
    • Lead weekly operations meetings and monthly business reviews
    • Track group-wide revenue, cost, and member lifecycle metrics
    • Develop and standardize replicable operating procedures (SOP)
    • Design cross-venue and cross-program member referral flows
  • 2. Sports Program Productization
    • Oversee curriculum and product design across all sports verticals (Basketball, Tennis, Pickleball, Sports Recovery)
    • Ensure product-market fit, quality standards, and tiered membership structures
    • Drive member lifecycle design and cross-program upsell and referral
  • 3. CrossFit / HYROX Business Line
    • Act as interim Division Director during the initial launch phase
    • Lead venue positioning, equipment configuration, and curriculum design
    • Recruit, train, and manage coaches for CrossFit and HYROX programs
    • Establish trial class processes, sales flows, and in-house competition systems
  • 4. Events, Competitions & Brand Partnerships
    • Organize inter-program competitions and member challenge events
    • Develop the Quanyu Group challenge event platform
    • Lead corporate wellness program development and enterprise sales
    • Identify and develop external brand partnership opportunities
  • 5. AI & Information Systems Implementation
    • Automate venue manager weekly reports and key operational workflows
    • Implement member CRM and trial-client tracking systems
    • Deploy operational data dashboard and churn prediction tools
    • Build AI-powered FAQ automation and customer service flows
    • Implement the AI Coach Teaching Evaluation System: coaches wear microphones during class; voice and video are recorded and analyzed by AI to evaluate teaching quality, instruction clarity, and coaching style; outputs are used for coach-member matching and periodic coach performance review
    • Lead AI literacy training for venue managers and division directors
  • 6. Executive Reporting
    • Provide regular operational progress and strategic updates to the CEO
    • Drive data-informed decision-making across all business lines

Work Arrangement & Autonomy

This is a full-responsibility, results-based arrangement. Once the goals, scope, and KPIs for the applicable phase have been mutually confirmed, the COO shall have full autonomy over working hours, work methods, and schedule needed to achieve the agreed outcomes. Performance shall be evaluated against agreed deliverables and KPIs rather than fixed attendance hours, provided the COO remains available for the operational cadence set out above (weekly operations meetings, monthly business reviews, and CEO reporting).

The Engagement Level percentages in the Compensation Structure table (50% / 75% / 100%) describe the relative scope of deliverables and KPIs assigned at each phase — they are not a measure of clocked hours, on-site attendance, or same-day on-call availability. The Company shall not expect the COO to be available for ad hoc operational matters beyond the deliverables, meeting cadence, and KPI scope defined for the applicable phase.

Addendum — Points for Clarification Prior to Signing

To ensure mutual understanding and smooth execution of the COO role, both parties agree to clarify and confirm the following eight points prior to formal signing.

This Addendum forms an integral part of this Offer Letter and carries the same binding effect as the main body above.

1
Compensation Aligned to Engagement Level
The three-phase base salary amounts (NT$90,000 → NT$140,000 → NT$180,000) correspond to the COO's increasing time investment and engagement level (50% → 75% → 100%), not to a performance-based or tenure-based raise. Each phase's salary takes effect automatically and unconditionally at its milestone, regardless of performance evaluation, and shall not be withheld, delayed, or suspended for performance reasons. Only the annual bonus, calculated based on KPI achievement, is performance-linked.
2
Phase 1–2 KPI Scope
During Months 1–4, performance shall be evaluated against process and deliverable KPIs — assessment, planning, and system-building progress — not full group-level revenue or membership targets, which presuppose full authority and full-time engagement.
3
Authority–Responsibility Alignment
The COO's authority over budget, headcount, data access, and cross-departmental coordination shall be set out in a written Delegation of Authority schedule — specifying budget figures, named reporting lines, and approval limits — signed concurrently with this Offer Letter. The COO shall not be expected to assume operational management responsibility, nor shall any KPI or performance expectation attach, before this schedule is signed.
4
CrossFit / HYROX New Business Line Compensation
Building CrossFit / HYROX from zero goes beyond standard COO management duties. Both parties shall negotiate in good faith and use reasonable efforts to reach a written bonus or profit-sharing framework by the end of Month 3. Until agreed, the COO shall not be expected to assume full P&L or outcome-based responsibility for this line beyond the base COO role. If no written agreement is reached by that date, the COO may pause further build-out work on this line (e.g. recruitment, curriculum design, equipment configuration) beyond ordinary base COO oversight, without this being treated as underperformance or grounds for withholding any compensation, until terms are agreed.
5
Independence of 四季健身 / 4SC CrossFit
四季健身 and 4SC CrossFit remain independent legal and operational entities. This appointment does not transfer any brand assets, member data, coach relationships, curriculum, SOP, or event IP to Quanyu Group. They may separately serve as an independent strategic partner of Quanyu Group where mutually beneficial, under a separate written agreement.
6
Written Authorization for Use of 四季 Resources
Any use of 四季健身 or 4SC CrossFit resources by Quanyu Group requires a separate written agreement defining scope, duration, cost allocation, revenue sharing, and exit mechanism.
7
AI Coach Evaluation System — Data Governance
Before any recording begins, both parties shall establish a written data governance policy — compliant with the Personal Data Protection Act and other applicable ROC laws — covering notification, consent, collection scope, storage, access control, permitted use, and deletion. No coach or member shall be recorded without prior notice and written consent.
8
Employment vs. Consulting Classification
Prior to signing, the parties shall clarify whether this appointment is an employment relationship or a consulting / contractor arrangement, and confirm the corresponding treatment of labor insurance, national health insurance, labor pension contributions, leave entitlements, tax withholding, and working-hour expectations.

Upon written acknowledgment of the above eight points, this offer letter shall be considered complete and ready for formal execution. We look forward to welcoming you to the Quanyu Group leadership team.
