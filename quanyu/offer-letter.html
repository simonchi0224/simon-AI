五、COO 職務範圍
  1. 集團營運整合：建立場館店長制度、項目總監制度、週會與月會節奏、營收與成本追蹤、會員生命周期與跨項目導流流程。
  2. 運動項目產品化：協助籃球、網球、匹克球、運動恢復、CrossFit / HYROX 等項目建立課程結構、會員方案、體驗流程、教練標準與營運 SOP。
  3. CrossFit / HYROX 新事業線：負責初期定位、場館配置、課程系統、教練招募與培訓、體驗課流程及賽事活動規劃，但其商品化、P&L、複製展店及分潤激勵依本協議第十一條另行處理。
  4. 活動、賽事與品牌合作：設計跨項目挑戰賽、會員活動、企業健康方案、外部品牌合作與集團級活動平台。
  5. AI 與資訊系統導入：推動營運儀表板、CRM、體驗追蹤、會員流失預警、客服 FAQ、自動化報告、AI 教練評估系統與管理者 AI 培訓。
  6. 高階經營報告：向 CEO 定期提出營運狀況、組織問題、策略建議、預算需求、風險事項與下一階段優先順序。
English Reference
  1. Group operations integration: venue manager systems, program director systems, weekly and monthly management cadence, revenue and cost tracking, member lifecycle, and cross-program referral flows.
  2. Sports program productization: course structure, membership plans, trial-class process, coaching standards, and operating SOPs across basketball, tennis, pickleball, sports recovery, CrossFit / HYROX, and other programs.
  3. CrossFit / HYROX new business line: initial positioning, venue configuration, course system, coach recruitment and training, trial-class process, and event planning, with commercialization, P&L, replication, and incentives handled under Article 11.
  4. Events, competitions, and brand partnerships: cross-program challenges, member events, corporate wellness programs, external brand partnerships, and a group-level event platform.
  5. AI and information systems: operations dashboards, CRM, trial tracking, churn prediction, FAQ automation, AI coach evaluation, and management AI training.
  6. Executive reporting: regular reporting to the CEO on operations, organizational issues, strategic recommendations, budget needs, risks, and next-stage priorities.

  1. 第 1-4 個月為導入期與轉換期，評估重點為診斷、盤點、制度設計、管理節奏建立、資料整合、Roadmap 與試行成果，不以完整集團營收或會員數結果作為單一評估標準。
  2. 第 5 個月起，雙方應依正式授權表、資料可得性、預算、人力與公司策略，確認 full-time COO KPI。
  3. 年度獎金不設定固定保證金額，應依乙方 KPI 達成度、公司績效及雙方另行確認之獎金制度計算。若乙方於年度中途離職、終止或不再擔任本職務，且非因重大違約或可歸責於乙方之終止，該年度獎金應依乙方實際任職期間、該期間 KPI 達成度及公司績效按比例計算，並於 KPI 與財務數字確認後三十日內結算；最遲不得晚於離職或終止日起六十日。
  4. 若 KPI 需依賴甲方內部主管、預算、資料或跨部門配合，應同步確認甲方提供條件；未提供條件者，不得單方歸責乙方。

  1. Months 1-4 are onboarding and transition phases. Evaluation shall focus on diagnosis, mapping, system design, management cadence, data integration, roadmap, and pilot progress, not full group-level revenue or membership outcomes.
  2. From Month 5 onward, full-time COO KPIs shall be confirmed based on the formal authority schedule, data availability, budget, personnel, and company strategy.
  3. Annual bonus is not a guaranteed fixed amount and shall be calculated based on Party B's KPI achievement, company performance, and the bonus system confirmed by the parties. Mid-year departure or termination not caused by Party B's material breach shall be prorated based on actual service period and KPI achievement, settled within thirty days after KPI and financial figures are confirmed, and no later than sixty days after departure or termination.
